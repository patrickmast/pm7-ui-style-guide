The elegant P direction: a flowing monogram with a restrained cream, gold and deep navy palette. Designed to remain recognizable as embroidery on a baseball cap and as a standalone personal mark.

P

Primary use

Single P centered on the front panel with generous space around the mark.

Embroidery

Use cream thread for the main form and a narrow warm-gold edge. Avoid extra fine interior details.

Small size

Keep the silhouette simple and recognizable. The P should read immediately from a distance.
